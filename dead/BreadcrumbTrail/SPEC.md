# BreadcrumbTrail

## Look
- Compact horizontal trail with slash, chevron, or dot separators
- Muted ancestor links; current page in white with violet underline accent
- Wraps gracefully on narrow viewports

## Page behavior
- Sits at top of content sections or below global nav
- Static — no scroll effects; low z-index

## Neighbors
- Above: site header or hero eyebrow
- Below: page title, filters, or main content grid

## Viewport and resize
- Mobile: wraps to multiple lines with gap-y
- Tablet/Desktop: single-line when space allows
- prefers-reduced-motion: underline is static (no animation needed)
