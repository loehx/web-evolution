# FolioMarginSplit

## Creative direction

**Style:** Editorial
**Typography:** Serif headlines, italic margin notes in small caps
**Layout:** Open folio spread — image on verso, copy on recto with red margin annotations
**Color:** Cream paper, ink black, crimson margin marks
**Motion:** Page-turn fade-in; margin notes stagger in
**Signature:** Red-ink marginalia running beside the body column like a marked-up manuscript

## Role

- image + text

## Look

- A manuscript folio: photograph on the left page, headline and body on the right with handwritten-style margin notes in the gutter.

## Motion

- Role: content
- Moves: fade-up, stagger, image-reveal
- Durations: standard for text, emphasis for image

## Page behavior

- Root: `min-h-[100svh] w-full`; two-page spread on desktop, stacked on mobile

## Neighbors

- Above: card slider or hero
- Below: contact or footer

## Width model

- Full viewport; inner columns have measure but root is full bleed

## Image ratios

- Folio image: 4/5 portrait crop

## Headlines

- Primary: ResponsiveHeadline with explicit lines

## Responsive

- Mobile: image top, copy below, margin notes inline
- Desktop: side-by-side folio spread with gutter annotations

## 3D

- None
