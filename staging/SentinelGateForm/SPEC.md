# SentinelGateForm

## Creative direction

**Style:** Brutalist
**Typography:** Heavy uppercase labels, monospace field hints
**Layout:** Massive iron gate frames the form; fields sit between vertical bars
**Color:** Gunmetal gray, rust orange accents, chalk white labels
**Motion:** Bars slide open on focus; submit is a heavy slab press
**Signature:** Contact fields threaded between brutalist gate bars that part when you type

## Role

- contact form

## Look

- A full-viewport iron gate with vertical bars; name, email, and message fields live in the gaps and the bars animate apart on focus.

## Motion

- Role: primary interaction
- Moves: slide-in, scale-in
- Durations: micro for focus, emphasis for submit

## Page behavior

- Root: `min-h-[100svh] w-full`; gate spans full width
